Founder & Director

Asha Dalavai

Asha Dalavai, Founder & Director of Trikuta Adventures & Sports Pvt. Ltd., is an adventure entrepreneur and mountaineer with experience in adventure tourism, project development, travel, and outdoor experiential programs.

Her professional journey includes adventure park project development and consulting, including work with Ramoji Film City on an adventure park project. She has also invested in and developed adventure tourism initiatives in collaboration with the Andhra Pradesh Forest Department, contributing to the development of outdoor adventure experiences and tourism infrastructure.

With experience spanning adventure projects, camps, trekking, school and corporate programs, and destination-based experiences, Asha is passionate about creating safe, innovative, and professionally managed adventure experiences that connect people with nature and encourage exploration, confidence, and personal growth.

Her vision Explore. Grow. Achieve.
