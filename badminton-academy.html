Technical Director & Head Coach

J. Suresh Babu

J. Suresh Babu is a former National-ranked badminton player with over 12 years of experience in badminton coaching and player development.

As the Technical Director & Head Coach at Trikuta Badminton Academy, he brings a strong combination of competitive experience, technical knowledge, and structured coaching methodology. His approach focuses on building strong fundamentals, improving technique and fitness, developing match strategies, and nurturing discipline and confidence in players.

With extensive experience working with players across different age groups and skill levels, Suresh Babu is committed to helping every player progress from the fundamentals to competitive-level badminton and achieve their individual sporting goals.
