How we work

Survey to handover

  1. 01Site survey. We walk the site, measure it and note what the ground and the access will allow.
  2. 02Design and costing. Layout, materials, safety standards and a line-item budget you can take to a board.
  3. 03Build. Fabrication, installation and inspection, with a single point of contact through the programme.
  4. 04Handover. Operating instructions, staff training and a maintenance schedule. We can run the site for you as well.
