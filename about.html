About Trikuta

One brand, four disciplines

Trikuta Adventures & Sports Pvt Ltd brings adventure, sports, travel experiences and infrastructure development together under one integrated brand.

Who we are

Run out of Electronic City

The company was set up to do four things properly rather than one thing loosely: coach badminton, run adventure programmes, plan travel, and build the facilities those activities need. Most operators do one of them and subcontract the rest.

Because the same team coaches, guides, supplies the gear and builds the site, a school or a company deals with one point of contact instead of four. That is the whole argument for the structure.

Our vision

To make the outdoors and organised sport a normal part of life in Bengaluru, not a once-a-year trip.

Our mission

Run safe, well-planned programmes at a price families and schools can actually pay. Coach players properly rather than quickly. Build facilities that are still standing and still safe in ten years.
